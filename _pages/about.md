## 👨🏻‍🔬 Selected Experience  

**Teciem (formerly Finastra), AI Software Engineer (Jan 2025 – Jul 2026)**  
I was part of the Core AI team working on Kondor Assistant, a chatbot that lets traders and analysts talk to a treasury and capital markets platform in plain English. I focused on the agent layer: a multi-agent ReAct system with API, SQL, semantic, and report agents, all coordinated by a top-level assistant agent. Most of my time went into making text-to-SQL reliable on a complex schema. I built a tool that works out the right join paths between tables, which cut token usage per query by about 30%. I also worked on the access controls around the AI-generated SQL, replaced an inconsistent LLM-based hyperlink feature with a deterministic pipeline, and turned 1,000+ messy QA cases into JSON eval sets with an agent, which cut manual annotation by about 95%. Response accuracy ended up at around 96%.

**Carnegie Mellon University, Computer Vision Research Intern (Sep 2024 – Jan 2025)**  
I worked on BOE-ViT, the first vision transformer designed for aligning 3D subtomograms from cryo-electron tomography. I helped design and implement two modules, Polyshift and MARE, which improve the model's shift and rotation equivariance. At low SNR, this gave 77% lower rotation error and 62% lower translation error. I also built the synthetic subtomogram generation and 2D-slice visualization pipelines, wrote a Fourier-domain missing-wedge mask and interpolation step to reduce artifacts, and debugged the training code so it could run at larger batch sizes and be reproduced reliably. I also contributed to the literature review, the experiments, and the PyTorch codebase. The paper was accepted at **CVPR 2025**.

**Nanyang Technological University, Speech & Language AI Intern (Mar 2024 – Aug 2024)**  
I worked on detecting and masking personally identifiable information (PII) in Singapore English speech. There was very little PII data to train on, so I used an LLM to generate synthetic text and WhisperSpeech to turn it into speech-text pairs. I then built an entity-aware ASR model trained jointly on ASR and NER, which beat the standard pipeline approach by 6.4% on PII detection accuracy. The system reached 0.79 F1 for detecting and masking PII in audio. This work led to four first-author publications: IALP 2024, TENCON 2024, ICAICTA 2024, and IJALP 2024.
